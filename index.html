NOT HALF. NOT LESS. WHOLE.

You fulfil
what I cannot be alone.

A waning gibbous and a waxing crescent are different phases of the same celestial body. They are not identical in light, shape, or stage — and that is what makes their meeting so beautiful.

Together, they become our symbol of the full Moon: not because either of us is incomplete, but because love gives two individual lives a shared wholeness neither could create alone.
