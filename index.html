Cloches du Mariage

Dr. Meghna & Bibin

Two Souls, One Sacred Journey

❧

As two hearts unite in the sacred bond of marriage, may the blessings of the Vedas illuminate your path. May your life together be filled with Dharma, Artha, Kama and Moksha — harmony, prosperity, love and spiritual fulfilment.

ॐ सह नाववतु । सह नौ भुनक्तु ।
सह वीर्यं करवावहै ॥

May the sacred fire bear witness to your vows, may every season deepen your companionship, and may love, grace and divine blessings guide Dr. Meghna and Bibin throughout a beautiful life together.
